How the score works

Every asset gets a 0–100 composite score from five parts, each scaled from −1 to +1. 50 is neutral.

  • Trend (25%): price above its 200-day average, 50-day above 200-day, and a rising 200-day average.
  • Momentum (20%): 12-month return (excluding the last month) and 3-month return, ranked against every other asset tracked.
  • Value (20%): how cheap the asset is compared with its own history: its stretch from the 200-day average as a percentile, its price relative to the S&P 500 as a 10-year percentile, and its distance from its all-time high.
  • Timing (15%): whether now is a good entry point. An oversold RSI inside an uptrend scores well; an overbought RSI scores badly.
  • Macro (20%): how the asset has tended to respond to current conditions such as the yield curve, credit spreads, recession risk, inflation, the dollar, real yields, fear and oil.

Ratings: Strong Buy ≥70 · Buy ≥60 · Hold ≥45 · Caution ≥35 · Avoid <35. Alerts fire when a rating changes, a timing setup appears (oversold uptrend, capitulation, golden or death cross, relative-value turn, breakout, overextension) or a macro threshold is crossed (VIX, credit spreads, yield curve, Sahm rule, Fed cuts, sentiment). A bullish setup that has historically underperformed for that asset is downgraded to Info.

Educational tool, not investment advice. Back-tests use a single ~10-year sample and past patterns may not repeat. Data: Yahoo Finance / Stooq (prices, dividend-adjusted where available) and FRED (macro). Updated automatically after the US market close.
